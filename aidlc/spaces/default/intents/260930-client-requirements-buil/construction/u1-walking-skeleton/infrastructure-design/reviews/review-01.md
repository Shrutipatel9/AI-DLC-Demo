### Summary

The pipeline enforces every gate the NFR design promises, the secret handling (throwaway keys, nothing committed, pull_request trigger, read-only token) is sound, and the traceability file is complete. The main architectural concerns are two Majors: the e2e job (and Compose credentials) is not specified in enough detail to run as written, and make verify diverges from CI, most seriously by running the migration downgrade against a developer's own database. Both are specification fixes, not design flaws, so the verdict is READY with no Critical findings and two Major findings; the nine Minor items are hardening and consistency points.
