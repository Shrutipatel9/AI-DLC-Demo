### Summary

The design is thorough and implementable. Workflows, state machines, entities and rules align with the client auth rules and the contract, and the slice is genuinely end-to-end. The two items to weigh before approval are the missing atomicity requirement on code verification (R-01), which affects the lockout and single-use guarantees, and the undefined code-retrieval path for the skeleton's end-to-end verification (R-02). The remaining findings are small clarifications.
