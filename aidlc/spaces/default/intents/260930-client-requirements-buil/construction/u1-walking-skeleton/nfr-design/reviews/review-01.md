### Summary
The design files are coherent on structure and cover all 58 requirement ids, and the commit, send, compensate flow and the separate-session limiter are sound. Two issues keep this from READY. The verify transaction diverges from the functional spec and leaves the counter row's creation and lock reset undefined, which creates an enumeration oracle and a first-insert race in the slice's core security control (R-01). The single 40-thread pool lets slow email sends starve verify, me and health (R-02). The traceability misses (R-03), the dev-mode CSP and App Router details (R-04), the logging allow-list conflict (R-05) and the undesigned /docs and /metrics handling (R-06) are also needed before code generation.
