### Summary
The NFR set is quantified, testable and consistent with the functional design rules and the contract, and the earlier open findings are genuinely addressed. Two Major gaps remain that should be fixed before code generation: the public allow-list does not match the health-ready and metrics routes added elsewhere (R-01), and the email send is not separated from the database lock and connection (R-02). Neither makes the slice unbuildable or insecure by itself, so the verdict is READY with 2 Major and 7 Minor findings.
