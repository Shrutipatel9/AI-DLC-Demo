### Summary
The NFR design maps nearly every requirement to a section and matches the contract and ADR-006, but its two central concurrency mechanisms are unsound as written. The single-statement revokes can miss a child created by a concurrent refresh, so logout and password reset do not reliably end a family. The recovery branch can hand out a cookie for an already rotated child. The anti-enumeration timing claim, the login-step lockout and the NFR9.9 accessibility design are also missing or unproven. Fix the five Major items (R-01 to R-05) and the Minor items, then re-review.
