### Summary

The contract set is thorough on client-stated auth, privacy and the shared error format, but four issues should be settled before approval: missing in-process contracts for the U8 -> U6/U7 and U10 -> U6 edges (R-01), an unspecified refresh-rotation race that will log users out in normal multi-tab use (R-02), no viable mechanism for displaying owner-checked media in the browser (R-03), and an error contract whose codes, statuses and generated types do not line up (R-04). The verdict is NOT-READY under the more-than-two-Major rule; it informs the human at the gate and does not block it.
