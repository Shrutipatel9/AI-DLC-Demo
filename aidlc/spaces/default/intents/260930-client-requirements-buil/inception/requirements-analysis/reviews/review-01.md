### Summary

The document is strong: client IDs are cited throughout, delivery-team decisions are labelled with the Q&A answers, scope is bounded, and most criteria are testable in Given/When/Then form. It is NOT-READY mainly because of three Major gaps. A known contradiction between reuse detection and session persistence is carried forward unresolved (R-01). The paid-path failure and repeat-purchase cases are unspecified (R-02). Password and upload limits are undefined (R-03). The human should weigh these at the gate; Request Changes would fix them through revisions.
